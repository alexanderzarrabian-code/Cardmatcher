Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Binder Buddy is a free app for keeping digital copies of your Pokémon card binders, looking up card prices, and trading with friends. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it. Questions: BinderBuddySupport@gmail.com.

What you can do without an account

Your binders, trade lists and wishlist are saved on your own phone, in the app's local storage. Nothing is sent to us unless you create an account and choose to share something, or you scan a card photo (see below).

What we store if you create an account

  • Sign-in details: your handle, and either your email with a password (stored only as a salted hash, never the password itself) or your Google account ID and email if you use Google sign-in.
  • Things you share: binders you set to Friends only or Public, trade lists, and your wishlist, with the visibility you chose. We never store what you paid for a card.
  • Social data: your friends, friend requests, people you have blocked, trade offers and requests, and the messages you send to friends.
  • Safety data: reports you send about other people, and reports others send about you, including a copy of the messages or offer involved.

Card photos

When you scan a card or a binder page, the photo is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service, which reads the card names and numbers. We do not save the photo on our server. Anthropic processes it under its own privacy terms.

Who can see what

  • Private binders and lists: only you.
  • Friends only: people you have accepted as friends.
  • Public: anyone using the app can browse them. Viewers see your handle, cards and market prices.
  • Messages and offers: only the two people involved. A report sends a copy to us.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts our server and database.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's web files.
  • Google, only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Anthropic, for reading card photos.
  • TCGplayer card images and price data are loaded to show cards and values.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Friends, Account, Delete my account. That permanently erases your profile, shared binders and lists, friends, offers, messages and sign-in data from our server. You can also ask us to do it by email, see the account deletion page. Copies of reports about you may be kept for a limited time to keep the community safe. Binders saved on your phone stay on your phone until you remove them.

Children

Binder Buddy is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
