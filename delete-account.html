Delete your account

Binder Buddy

In the app (fastest)
  1. Open Binder Buddy and go to the Friends tab.
  2. Tap Account at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and handle, and your sign-in details (email and password hash, or Google ID).
  • Your shared binders, trade lists and wishlist.
  • Your friends, friend requests and blocked list.
  • Your trade offers, requests and messages.

This happens right away and cannot be undone. Your handle becomes available to others again.

What is kept

Binders and lists saved on your own phone are not on our server, so they stay on your phone until you remove them. Safety reports that mention you may be kept for a limited time to protect the community.

Can't get into the app?

Email BinderBuddySupport@gmail.com from the address on your account, or include your handle, and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm by reply.
